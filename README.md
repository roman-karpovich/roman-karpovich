## ⚒️ Currently forging: [Kanatoko](https://github.com/roman-karpovich/kanatoko)

The tool I wished existed every time a Soroban contract had to meet mainnet:
pull the real network into an ordinary Rust test and find out what happens
*before* anything is deployed. Grew out of years of working against live Stellar state, and keeps
in step with every protocol upgrade.

## What I'm good at

- **Smart contracts** — the core contracts behind some of the largest and most critical DeFi protocols on Stellar: a large body of production Soroban code, taken through multiple independent audits. Solidity and Solana as well.
- **DeFi systems** — the backend side of on-chain protocols: liquidity and incentive mechanics, indexing chain state into data services can trust.
- **Rust where it matters** — code where both milliseconds and correctness count.
- **Blockchain internals** — how transactions are simulated, ordered and paid for, from the SDK down to the node.
- **AI-assisted engineering** — two generations of my own agent toolkit ([ai-dev-team](https://github.com/roman-karpovich/ai-dev-team)): spec-first development where one model builds and a model from another provider cross-reviews, with disagreements settled on evidence. Shipped and hardened production code with it.
- **Security** — reading contracts adversarially; LLM-assisted review.
- **Infrastructure** — AWS, Docker, Ansible, observability, and keeping the cloud bill honest.

Before crypto: a decade of Python/Django, much of it on **UNICEF eTools** — a large production
platform for UNICEF's programme and partner management. 1,600+ commits, one of its top five contributors,
plus the [open-source Django packages](https://github.com/orgs/unicef/repositories?q=unicef-) extracted from it.
